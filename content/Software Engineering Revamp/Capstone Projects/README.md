## How this chapter works

Unlike the teaching units, this chapter is not a sequence of taught topics. Each entry is a single extended project brief, worked through in stages that mirror the course's own progression: scope and process, requirements, planning, modelling, architecture and design, code quality, testing, release, operation and maintenance, quality and security, and professional practice.

The Software Engineering course is conceptual and contains no code, so the capstone produces engineering artefacts rather than a program. Its distinctive requirement is traceability: every requirement must connect to a model, a design decision, a test and a release step. The Mini Projects chapter gives practice with individual artefacts, and the capstone is where they have to agree with one another.
