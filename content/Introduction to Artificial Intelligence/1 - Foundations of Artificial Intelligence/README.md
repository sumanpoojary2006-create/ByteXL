# Unit 1: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

**Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**

Goal of this unit: explain the foundational concepts, evolution, applications, and ethical considerations of Artificial Intelligence.

Key question this unit answers: **What makes a machine intelligent?**

Every topic in this unit exists to answer that question more completely. The unit opens by separating intelligence from mere automation, traces how the field arrived at today's systems, maps the branches and types of AI, contrasts the two great families of approach, then turns outward to where AI is actually deployed, what responsible deployment demands, and where the field is heading next.
