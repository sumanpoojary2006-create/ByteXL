# Unit 6: Modern Artificial Intelligence

**Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**

Goal of this unit: analyze modern AI technologies, including Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, and their responsible deployment in real-world applications.

Key question this unit answers: **How are intelligent systems built, deployed, and applied in the real world?**

The unit has three movements. It opens with the established application areas, language, vision, speech, recommendation, and robotics, each showing how the techniques from earlier units are assembled into something people actually use. It then turns to the systems that have reshaped the field most recently: generative models, large language models, multimodal systems, and agents that plan and use tools. It closes with what deployment demands rather than what capability allows, covering explainability, safety, the lifecycle an AI project actually follows, and where the field appears to be heading.
