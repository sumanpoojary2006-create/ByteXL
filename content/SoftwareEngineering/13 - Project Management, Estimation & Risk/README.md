# Unit 13: Project Management, Estimation & Risk

Learn how software work is planned, estimated, de-risked, organised, tracked, and aligned with the people who depend on it: the project constraints, work breakdown and scheduling, size and effort estimation, algorithmic cost models, risk management, team organisation and productivity, progress tracking and metrics, stakeholder management, outsourcing and contracts, and product thinking. Conceptual and theory-focused: no code examples. Source of truth: `Course_Blueprint_for_Software_Engineering.xlsx`, sheet `Syllabus`, Unit 13.

3 chapters, 10 topics.
