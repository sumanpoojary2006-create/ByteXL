Course Builder

A tactical course is a college syllabus, not a new curriculum. Pick the blueprint, see exactly which published ByteXL lesson fills each syllabus topic and which topics nothing in the catalogue covers, then create the product with its units, chapters and topics already in the right positions.

1 · Choose a blueprint
Blueprints live in blueprints/ and pin every syllabus topic to a donor page id. Loading a plan re-reads those pages from ByteXL, so a renamed or gutted donor shows up here rather than in the college's course.

ByteXL holds two levels, so each chapter becomes one section titled unit.chapter - Chapter name and every topic becomes a page inside it. Click a unit to fold it.
4 · Create the reading material
This writes the lesson content only — a product under Reading Material, not a course. Donor bodies are copied, not linked, since ByteXL cannot share a page between products. Step 5 below turns this content into an actual course.
5 · Publish the course
This is the object that shows on ByteXL's own Course Builder screen: a Module → Chapter → Lesson tree. It creates no content — every lesson links straight into the donor course the material already lives in, so there is only ever one copy to maintain. Chapters that share a courseChapter in the blueprint merge here, while the reading material keeps the syllabus split. A topic with no donor is simply absent.
